A FEW USEFUL THINGS

Good to
know.

Simple answers for a considered visit.

How does the four-floor store work?＋

Choose a floor in the house guide. The ground level is the welcome and new arrivals; the three upper levels hold The Edit, After Hours, and The Studio. The illustrated staircase includes a shared landing, and a lift serves all levels.

How do I choose my size?＋

Open a product and use its size guide. The current catalogue is a visual demo, so fit notes and measurements are illustrative and will be replaced with verified product measurements.

Can I place a real order today?＋

Not yet. The bag and checkout preview work in this prototype, but payments, order fulfilment, and the live product database are not connected.

Where do the product images come from?＋

The starter catalogue uses illustrative editorial photography. Before launch, each colour and product variant needs its own verified image set from approved sources.
